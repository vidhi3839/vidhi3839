## About Me

I am an **MS Data Science student at Northeastern University** focused on applying data science to **product, customer, and business decision-making**.

My work combines **SQL, Python, statistical analysis, experimentation, machine learning, and data visualization** to answer questions such as:

- Where are customers dropping off in a product funnel?
- Which customers are most likely to churn?
- How should customers be segmented for targeted engagement?
- Which product or campaign changes actually improve outcomes?
- How can analytical results be translated into decisions that business teams can act on?

I have experience working with **250K+ CRM records, 100K+ transaction records, 50K–80K operational records, and 20K+ customer feedback comments**, building analytical solutions across customer intelligence, experimentation, segmentation, NLP, and business reporting.

## Featured Projects

### 1. [Product Analytics Case Study — Reducing Checkout Abandonment](#)

`BigQuery` `SQL` `Python` `Power BI` `A/B Testing` `Statistics`

Analyzed **4.3M+ Google Analytics 4 events across 333K+ user sessions** using BigQuery SQL and Python to understand the purchase funnel. Built session-level funnel analysis segmented by user type, device, geography, and acquisition channel to identify checkout friction and conversion opportunities.

Applied **Chi-square tests, Mann–Whitney U tests, and 95% confidence intervals** to evaluate conversion differences and designed a production-style A/B experiment with success metrics, guardrails, power analysis, MDE, and sample-size calculations.

**Focus:** Product Analytics · Funnel Analysis · Experimentation · Statistical Testing · KPI Design

---

### 2. [Customer Churn Prediction](#)

`Python` `Scikit-learn` `Random Forest` `Customer Analytics`

Built a **Random Forest churn prediction model achieving 0.87 ROC-AUC** using demographic, contract, and billing features. Performed exploratory analysis and feature engineering to identify the strongest behavioral and customer-level drivers associated with churn.

Evaluated model performance using **ROC-AUC and F1-score** and ranked customer churn probabilities to create a risk-based segmentation approach for targeted retention campaigns.

**Focus:** Customer Analytics · Predictive Modeling · Feature Engineering · Retention Strategy

---

### 3. [Retail Demand Forecasting & Price Optimization](#)

`Python` `PyTorch` `Time Series` `Feature Engineering` `Optimization`

Processed **1.06M+ retail transactions** into 159K+ weekly product records across 3,139 products and engineered 30 time-series features including lag variables, rolling statistics, seasonality, and price elasticity.

Developed and compared **5 forecasting models**, with a PyTorch Deep Neural Network achieving **R² = 0.9513** on a temporal holdout dataset. Built a demand-based price optimization framework and evaluated prediction uncertainty using bootstrap sampling.

**Focus:** Forecasting · Time-Series Modeling · Pricing Analytics · Decision Intelligence

---

### 4. [AI Resume Matching Platform](#)

`Python` `Sentence-BERT` `Flask` `React` `Gemini API`

Fine-tuned **Sentence-BERT on 9,544 expert-labeled resume–job description pairs**, improving semantic matching performance to **R² = 0.6964** and outperforming TF-IDF, pretrained SBERT, and Random Forest baselines.

Built an end-to-end application using **Flask, React, and Gemini API** for automated resume parsing, semantic matching, and recruiter-facing candidate ranking.

**Focus:** NLP · Semantic Search · Model Evaluation · Applied ML · Full-Stack ML
